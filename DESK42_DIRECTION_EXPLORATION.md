## Active candidate directions

### Candidate 1 — The Living Office

The player leaves or moves beyond Desk 42 and navigates a mutable bureaucratic environment. Regulations alter access, routes, ownership, threats, and spatial rules.

Open questions:

- What remains uniquely Desk 42 rather than becoming a stealth or exploration game in office clothing?
- What does leaving the post cost, and why is that transgression pleasurable?
- How do processing, management, builds, action, and fugue feed one another?

### Candidate 2 — The Company Machine

The player manages an interconnected organisation in which departments, employees, anomalies, evidence, resources, and regulations create autonomous cascades.

Open questions:

- How can it avoid visual and mechanical collision with Lobotomy Corporation?
- What is the player’s immediate verb rather than their role as an observer of meters?
- Can its strongest management ideas live inside another direction without inheriting the facility-sim structure?

### Candidate 3 — Claim Dive / Legal Reality

The player enters claims, extracts incomplete evidence, files an official ruling, and creates precedents that may alter future reality.

Open questions:

- Is entering an action arena genuinely the best delivery mechanism?
- Can legal-state manipulation remain readable under action pressure?
- Does the ruling remain the spine, or can it exist inside a broader form of play?
- Can Reality Debt become chosen power rather than disguised punishment?
- Does this direction contain enough management simulation without becoming a second, disconnected game?
