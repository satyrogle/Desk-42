Department Grammar

Six departments, six physical verbs

Intake - Separate

Fan trays, scanner apertures and many arrivals becoming one ordered line. Dusty blue glass and wire baskets.

Verification - Compare

Paired lightbeds, split shutters and contradiction needles. Amber light stays local to evidence.

Adjudication - Commit

The branch's strongest downward motion: heavy stamp press, sealed gate and restrained red at impact.

Archive - Retrieve

Visible capacity, index drums, mobile shelving and old information becoming physically harder to reach.

Legal - Loop

Reverse tubes, bound briefs, hearing clocks and locked bays for test cases. Error returns here.

Payroll - Pulse

Punch clocks, rota wheels and predictable staff availability. Breaks are visible before they happen.

A department must remain recognisable in greyscale. Accent colour is the sixth cue, not the first.

What worked

Blender-guided SDXL ControlNet preserved filing-cabinet structure and produced repeatable candidate pixels. Contact sheets, manifests, validation and dry-run promotion exist.

What did not

Character identity-safe editing was not validated. The selected cabinet still needs native-grid redraw. Technical checks never make raw generated art shippable.

Kill the direction if

  • A route cannot be traced in one second.
  • Departments need colour or labels to read.
  • An appeal resembles ordinary red loot.
  • Machines look like factory boxes wearing paper.
  • Aliens look like collectible mascots.
  • Complexity becomes visual noise before it becomes gameplay.
  • AI correction costs more than direct pixel redraw.
  • The final empty desk does not echo the built branch.
The first public-quality frame must show that the branch manufactured a bad decision and the decision has returned to demand processing.
