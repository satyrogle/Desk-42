# Desk42 Persistent Institutional Feed v0.3

## Status

Playable product-depth checkpoint on
`codex/persistent-institutional-feed-v0.3`.

## Accurate checkpoint statement

Desk42 v0.3 is a playable institutional automation blockout in which one
continuing eight-agent society supplies work to the physical processing floor.
Agent decisions create material events, observable records and admitted cases.
Factory rulings alter that retained society, later operating batches can contain
cases caused by earlier rulings, and returned appeals commit appellate rulings
and holdings in the same authority-owned docket.

This checkpoint does not claim a general AI storyteller, a production-complete
society simulation, or commercial content breadth.

## Product loop

```text
one retained society
-> autonomous action
-> material event
-> public observation
-> admitted dossier
-> physical factory routing
-> evidence-driven automated ruling
-> executable remedy and scope
-> later autonomous consequences
-> descendant case or appeal
-> appellate ruling and optional holding
-> later matching case may cite that holding
```

The factory remains the playable surface. The institutional engine supplies the
work and executes consequences underneath it.

## Implemented depth

### Persistent society feed

- All eight shifts use one `InstitutionalAutomationSession`.
- Claim IDs and case lineage continue across shift boundaries.
- Dossiers are generated by the generic decision, material-event, observation
  and docket services rather than by cloning the canonical one-case slice.
- The feed currently produces possession disputes and documented access
  withdrawals.
- Visible transfers between agents alter trust and fear through generic action
  rules.
- Broad possession rulings can create traceable later cases in a subsequent
  operating batch.

### Evidence-driven automatic adjudication

- Public documentary sources have deterministic support differences: camera,
  access-log and damaged-sensor records are not treated as equivalent.
- Proof Fortress uses the highest support requirement, sends weak records to a
  physical Legal hold, and uses narrow scope.
- Rubber Mill uses a low support threshold and broad scope.
- Appeal Refinery uses a moderate threshold, broad scope and a specialised
  appellate path.
- Recognised possession remedies restore the resource to its registered owner.
- Recognised access remedies restore the authority-owned access grant exactly
  once, even if an autonomous agent later withdraws it again.

### Backend appellate resolution

- A returned appeal is filed against a specific descendant case and challenged
  ruling.
- Full rehearing, fast track and settlement are recorded as appellate procedure.
- Resolution commits an appellate ruling into the endogenous docket.
- Eligible non-settlement resolutions install a real holding.
- The HUD precedent count is derived from the holding registry.
- Precedent Reuse matches a real holding to a later case and records the case on
  that holding; it no longer increments a factory-local counter.

### Institutional procedures

The two-slot factory loadout retains its physical effects and is also recorded
on committed institutional rulings:

- Mandatory Secondary Verification
- Presumption of Validity
- Automatic Adverse Review
- Protected Evidence Channel
- Appeal Fast Track
- Precedent Reuse

## Architecture boundary

```text
Domain      -> generic agents, decisions, rulings and scope vocabulary
Authority   -> material truth, docket, remedies, appeals and holdings
Player      -> public-safe persistent automation session
Product     -> Unity floor, routing, machines, HUD and input
```

`Desk42.Product` continues to reference `Desk42.Institutional.Player` only. It
does not acquire direct access to Domain or Authority state.

## Automated evidence

- `InstitutionalAutomationSessionTests`: persistent shifts, public evidence
  variation, descendant work, access restoration, real appellate holdings,
  precedent matching and procedure recording.
- Complete EditMode suite exercises schema, deep-copy, snapshot, persistence,
  decision and validation regressions.
- Active-product PlayMode suite exercises scene boot, build changes, overload,
  repair, physical secondary verification and a physical appeal that installs a
  backend holding.
- The archived causal-legibility scene tests remain categorised
  `ArchivedCausalLegibility` and are excluded from the active product run.

## Current limits

- The society remains a bounded eight-agent product slice, not an unbounded
  population simulation.
- Possession and access are the active case families; collective grievance is
  supported by the engine but is not yet deliberately surfaced by this product
  seed.
- Automated policy profiles use explicit support thresholds rather than learned
  adjudication.
- Automation-session persistence across application restarts is not yet exposed
  by the product.
- Art and audio remain blockout production quality.
