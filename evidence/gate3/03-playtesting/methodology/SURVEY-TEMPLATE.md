# Desk 42 — Playtest Survey Template

Administered to every tester after their session(s). Covers four areas: understanding, experience, differentiation, and commercial signal.

---

## Section 1: Understanding

1. **What did you think the game was about?** *(open text)*

2. **Was anything unclear when you first started?** *(open text)*

## Section 2: Experience

3. **What part did you find most engaging?** *(open text)*

4. **Where did you feel confused, frustrated, or bored?** *(open text)*

5. **Was there any point where you considered stopping? If so, what was happening?** *(open text)*

6. **How would you rate the overall experience?** *(1–5 scale: 1 = Poor, 5 = Excellent)*

7. **How would you rate the difficulty?** *(1–5 scale: 1 = Too easy, 3 = Just right, 5 = Too hard)*

## Section 3: Differentiation

8. **What, if anything, felt different from other games you have played?** *(open text)*

9. **How would you describe Desk 42 to another person?** *(open text)*

## Section 4: Commercial Signal

10. **Would you consider playing the finished version?** *(Yes / Maybe / No)*

11. **Would you wishlist the game after playing this build?** *(Yes / Maybe / No)*

12. **What would most affect that decision?** *(open text)*

## Section 5: Technical

13. **Did you encounter any bugs or crashes?** *(Yes / No — if yes, describe)*

14. **Any other feedback?** *(open text)*

---

## Administration notes

- All open-text responses are preserved verbatim in raw survey files. Never edit raw responses.
- Processed/coded versions go in `../analysis/`.
- Include tester ID (D42-Txxx) and build version on each response.
